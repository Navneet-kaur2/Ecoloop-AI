⚡ How Generation is Estimated

We use the standard photovoltaic output formula:

Daily Output = Capacity (kW) × Peak Sun Hours × (1 − Losses)

  • 🌞 Peak Sun Hours: Typically 4.5 to 5.5 hours/day across Punjab and North India.
  • 📉 System Losses: Accounts for inverter efficiency, wiring, dust, and temperature (~15–20%).
  • 💰 Net Metering: Excess units exported back to the grid reduce monthly bills.
